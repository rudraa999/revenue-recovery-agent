Shipping Policy

1. Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1-3 business days. Orders are not shipped or delivered on weekends or holidays.

If we are experiencing a high volume of orders, shipments may be delayed by a few days. Please allow additional days in transit for delivery. If there will be a significant delay in shipment of your order, we will contact you via email or telephone.

2. Shipping Rates & Delivery Estimates

Shipping charges for your order will be calculated and displayed at checkout.

  • Standard Delivery: 3-5 business days.
  • Express Delivery: 1-2 business days.
  • Overnight Delivery: Next morning delivery for orders placed before 2 PM.

Delivery delays can occasionally occur due to unforeseen external circumstances.

3. Restrictions

We currently do not ship outside the contiguous United States. We do not ship to P.O. boxes or APO/FPO addresses.

4. Damages

The Arts Arcade is not liable for any products damaged or lost during shipping. If you received your order damaged, please contact the shipment carrier to file a claim. Please save all packaging materials and damaged goods before filing a claim.
